# Potential Songs

This file is the canonical backlog and prioritization dossier for undeveloped and in-development song concepts in the Music Composition project.

It is governed by [Music & Song Framework](music-song-framework.md). The framework owns reusable songwriting and scoring rules; this file owns the actual multi-song concept catalog, candidate notes, prioritization, and status.

## Rating provenance

The original concept discussion used a table built around **what the child hears**, **what the adult hears**, and a star-based **Potential** rating.

The repository preserved the five leading concepts as **very high potential**, corresponding to ★★★★★, while the remaining concepts were preserved collectively as **additional strong concepts** without their individual historical star counts. Rather than invent historical precision, this dossier currently normalizes that second group to ★★★★☆.

These stars are concept-priority heuristics, not final song-quality scores. When choosing what to develop next, use the framework's current hard gates and weighted scoring model.

## Active concept — The Dishes Come Back

Discovery is complete and the song has moved into active development.

Canonical song-specific decisions now live in [**The Dishes Come Back**](songs/the-dishes-come-back.md).

Catalog summary:
- first-person household dishwasher;
- female lead vocal, with gender-neutral lyrics;
- pointed but affectionate satire of family cleanup dynamics;
- children contribute to the accumulating dishes and become comically unavailable at cleanup time;
- partner unavailability functions as a recurring adult-layer running gag;
- folksy country / Americana story-song lane;
- simple child-singable hook: **“The dishes come back / They always come back.”**
- false-victory ending: clean sink, brief silence, *clink*, then **“They always come back.”**

## Graduated / established concept

[**Woodpeckers Are the Best**](songs/woodpeckers-are-the-best.md) has moved beyond the potential-song backlog into an established song dossier. It remains a useful reference point for Pure Delight / Moment Song comedy and child-forward acoustic songwriting.

## Catalog maintenance

When a concept becomes an active song:
1. create or update its song-specific dossier under `docs/songs/`;
2. change its status here;
3. keep only catalog-level summary information here;
4. move song-specific decisions, lyrics, generation directions, and listening history into the song dossier.

When a new concept is added, capture enough information to distinguish its child layer, adult layer, primary mode, and likely catalog contribution before prioritizing it.
